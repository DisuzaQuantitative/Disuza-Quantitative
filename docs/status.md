## CURRENT — research foundation

The initiative is founder-led between Madrid, Spain, and Bizerte, Tunisia.

The current programme includes:

- rule-based strategy research;
- private research and engineering tooling;
- data acquisition and quality-control work;
- cloud-supported batch research workflows;
- statistical validation and reproducibility controls;
- audit-oriented research governance.

These capabilities support research. They do not establish a production
trading platform.

## IN PROGRESS — control strengthening

The programme is strengthening the controls that govern research access,
trial accounting, reproducibility, and audit evidence.

During this work, public documentation does not present a strategy as
qualified for deployment and does not make a current performance claim.
Completion requires evidence, not a documentation update.

## TARGET — qualified deployment path

Longer-term targets include:

- paper-trading qualification;
- venue-specific execution qualification;
- runtime risk and state controls;
- reconciled order and fill accounting;
- carefully governed direct or programme-based deployment.

These are target capabilities. They are not statements of current operation,
availability, or future results.

## Scope boundaries

The core research universe is:

- BTC and ETH perpetual markets;
- NQ and ES index-futures markets;
- optional CFD adaptations evaluated separately.

The primary design horizon is approximately 30 minutes to 4 hours. Research
scope may change through documented internal decisions, but public status is
updated only after those decisions are verified and safe to disclose.

## No performance or access claim

This public reference makes no claim of current strategy performance, public
platform access, investor-capital acceptance, or an investment service.
